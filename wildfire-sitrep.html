Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Breezy southerly winds persist across much of the central and southern Plains, especially central/western Oklahoma and the Texas Panhandle where gusts up to 35 mph will create elevated to locally critical conditions amid very hot temperatures and low RH. Locally breezy winds and low RH generally 10-20% will also create elevated conditions across parts of the northern Great Basin into northeast California and southeastern Oregon. Strong westerly winds into the northern Plains with occasional gusts exceeding 40 mph across parts of eastern Montana into North Dakota. More robust monsoon moisture plume into Arizona and western New Mexico extending into much of the northern and eastern Great Basin and greater Four Corners and West Slope, resulting in scattered to numerous mixed wet/dry thunderstorms. Isolated to scatted mixed wet/dry thunderstorms east of the Divide in Colorado, southern Wyoming extending into the central Plains. Isolated to scattered thunderstorms develop over parts of central and south Texas, becoming more numerous along the Gulf Coast, Florida and southeast Atlantic coast.
