A ridge of high pressure building in the wake of Isaias will bring hot, unstable, and windy conditions to northern Louisiana and east Arkansas today. A trough of low pressure over the inland Northwest will weaken today, though below normal temperatures will continue there and across the Great Basin with widespread precipitation across the Great Basin as well. Showers and isolated thunderstorms continue across southern California along with south to west winds of 20 to 30 mph with gusts to 50 mph across the mountain ridges and desert passes.
