Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Persistent very hot conditions are expected across southeast and south-central portions of the US where high temperatures will surge into the upper 90s to mid 100s. This will coincide with RHs in the teens and afternoon wind gusts of 20-30 mph resulting in high-risk triggers for significant fire. Hot, unstable, and potentially breezy conditions from thunderstorms could also develop over the lower Mississippi Valley. Over the western US, the main concern will revolve around very warm, dry and breezy conditions forming in the afternoon and evening across portions of the Great Basin, northeast California and the Cascade gaps in eastern Oregon and Washington. The latter locations will see wind gusts of 30-40 mph with RH values in the teens during the afternoon and early evening resulting elevated fire weather concerns. Over Alaska, most of the state will become cooler and wetter except for the Tanana Valley where precipitation isn’t expected at this time. Elsewhere in the US, the main feature will be very hot temperatures over the Desert Southwest and a strong cold front sweeping southward from the northern Plains. The front will deliver cooling temperatures and scattered thunderstorms across the Upper Midwest to the western Great Lakes.
