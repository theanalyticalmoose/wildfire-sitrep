Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

An upper-level low will slowly move eastward through the central Rockies today with widespread showers, falling as snow above 7,000 to 8,000 feet. To the east, a slow-moving cold front will continue to bring widespread showers and thunderstorms to the central Plains and Mid-Mississippi Valley today, with widespread showers and thunderstorms also expected in west Texas today as tropical moisture from Mexico and Pacific Hurricane Simon moves northward. A cold front will also move through the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic with light precipitation while the Southeast and Lower Mississippi Valley are dry. Dry weather is expected across most of the rest of the West, with above-normal temperatures expected across much of the West Coast. Light, but dry trade winds are expected in Hawai’i while cool temperatures and isolated snow showers are expected in Interior Alaska.
