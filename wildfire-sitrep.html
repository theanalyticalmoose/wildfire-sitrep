Predictive Services Discussion

Dry southwesterly winds 12-20 mph with gusts 20-35 mph will develop across southern Nevada into southwest Utah amid minimum relative humidity of 10-20% creating areas of elevated conditions. Lighter winds are expected in the northwestern U.S., although breezy west winds will continue near and east of the Divide in Montana and Wyoming. Dry conditions are expected in California with temperatures remaining slightly above normal for the southern half of the state. Drier conditions are expected in New Mexico today with only isolated showers while Arizona is mild and dry. Above normal temperatures will continue in the southern Plains to Lower Mississippi Valley but with light winds and minimum relative humidity near or above 30%. Dry northerly winds around 10 mph will also occur farther east into Georgia and the Carolinas. A strong Nor’easter will remain off the Mid-Atlantic and southern New England coast with strong northeast winds and coastal flooding, while heavy rain is also expected for Long Island and southern/eastern New England. Heavy rain will persist across Maui and the Big Island today along with strong winds for the Big Island as Hurricane Nolo passes south of the islands. Areas of flooding, perhaps locally catastrophic, will occur across Maui and the Big Island, as well.
