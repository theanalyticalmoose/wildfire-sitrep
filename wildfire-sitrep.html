Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Breezy west-southwest winds with gusts of 35–45 mph and RH of 5–20% will create elevated fire conditions across the Inland Northwest, northern Rockies, and northern Great Basin. Locally critical conditions are possible from northeast California and northwest Nevada into southeast Oregon and southwest Idaho. Scattered mixed wet/dry thunderstorms are expected across western Montana, central/eastern Idaho, and the central Rockies, with drier storms possible in northeast Washington and north Idaho. Isolated mixed wet/dry thunderstorms will also extend from the eastern Sierra through the Great Basin to the West Slope, while wetter storms remain more numerous in Colorado and New Mexico east of the Divide, and the western High Plains. Thunderstorms are expected from south Texas to the Southeast ahead of a slow-moving cold front, with thunderstorm activity increasing into the Mid-Atlantic and portions of the Northeast. Dry, seasonable weather will prevail across the Midwest and Great Lakes.

SPC Day 1 Fire Weather Outlook

Issued 1056 AM CDT Thu Aug 27 2026  ·  Valid 271700Z - 281200Z  ·  Mirrored live from spc.noaa.gov
CRITICALFAR NORTHEASTERN CALIFORNIA
CRITICALAND SOUTHEASTERN OREGON

No changes were made to the existing drawn fire weather risk areas for today. Satellite imagery and GLM data shows thunderstorm development already underway across portions of western MT. Precipitation already received in the last 24 hours and resultant ERCs below the 90th percentile, especially where the latest forecast guidance shows convection to be most widespread today, has precluded the introduction of any additional dry thunderstorm area(s) of concern. Meanwhile, much of the drawn wind/RH areas show transient scattered cloud cover this morning. Even so, enough solar radiation is still expected to mix down stronger winds from aloft, aiding the tightening surface pressure gradient in place across highlighted areas of the Pacific Northwest. The area of Critical concern, near the low elevations of southeastern OR, remains on track with sustained southwesterly winds of 15-25 mph amid RHs of 10-15% through several hours this afternoon ahead of an advancing cold front. The front will move across central OR and WA today and approach the Critical wind/RH area between sunset and midnight tonight as it slows, turning winds west-northwesterly as it passes.
