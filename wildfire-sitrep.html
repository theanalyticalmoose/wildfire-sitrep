Predictive Services Discussion

Widespread critical conditions will develop across the Inland Northwest into the northern Rockies and northwestern Great Basin today from westerly winds 15-30 mph with gusts 30-45 mph amid relative humidity of 5-15%. Elevated conditions from slightly weaker westerly winds are expected in central Idaho to southwest and central Montana, as well, where temperatures will be up to 20°F above normal. Very hot, record-setting temperatures 100-110°F are expected in southeast Montana into the western Dakotas with relative humidity below 20%, and isolated to scattered mixed wet and dry thunderstorms will develop resulting in several new ignitions. Hot and dry conditions are expected in much of central and southern California, while isolated mixed wet and dry thunderstorms develop in central Nevada to southeast Idaho and northern Utah. Isolated wet monsoon thunderstorms are expected in the Southwest into the central Rockies. Farther east, breezy conditions and low relative humidity and hot temperatures will support elevated fire weather conditions across the southern High Plains. In Minnesota, breezy northwest wind are expected amid hot temperatures and relative humidity of 25-40%. Isolated to scattered showers and thunderstorms will develop in the Great Lakes and Southeast, with scattered thunderstorms in Florida capable of new ignitions due to the ongoing long-term drought.
