Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Strong high pressure will drift westward into Desert Southwest today resulting in subtle warming and drying across much of the western US. Warmer than normal temperatures are forecast for most of the West highs in the upper 80s and 90s across most of the West with readings in the 100s in the Interior Valley of California and into western Arizona. This will combine with relative humidity values in the single digits and teens. Winds will generally be less than 15 mph. The shifting ridge will also allow a cold front to drop into the Central Plains delivering numerous thunderstorms as well as easing the hot and dry conditions of late, especially across eastern Colorado, Kansas and Missouri. South of the front, across Texas, Oklahoma, and Arkansas the oppressive heat will continue with highs forecast in the 100s and relative humidity values in the teens. South winds gusts of 20 to 25 mph in these areas will result in enhanced fire weather conditions. The other area of concern will lie over southern Florida where scattered thunderstorms over record dry fuels could result in new fire starts and gusty outflow winds.
