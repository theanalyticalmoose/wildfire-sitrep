Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Isolated to scattered mixed wet/dry thunderstorms are expected across portions of central/southern California, Nevada, Utah, Idaho, western Montana and Wyoming. Central and northern California will have increased risk of isolated dry lightning, elevating potential, while thunderstorms will be more numerous and wetter near the Sierra Crest. Low RH of 10–25% and gusty winds are expected across the Great Basin and into Wyoming and the West Slope, with wind gusts up to 45 mph possible in southern and western Wyoming. In the Southwest, isolated to scattered mixed wet/dry thunderstorms will favor Arizona and eastern/southern New Mexico. Across the eastern U.S., conditions will remain generally dry and seasonable for most areas, with scattered thunderstorms possible along the East Coast and across portions of the Southeast. Isolated to scattered thunderstorms are also expected across southern Texas and the Lower Mississippi Valley. Tropical Storm Dolly is expected to weaken while approaching the northeastern Caribbean, but associated moisture may increase showers and thunderstorms across Puerto Rico, but impacts remain uncertain.
