Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Humidity will slowly moderate across the Inland Northwest and northern Rockies with near to slightly above normal temperatures (10-20% minimum RH) and fair overnight recovery, resulting in shorter burn periods. Conversely, inland California, the northern Great Basin, and much of Wyoming will see persistent very low RH (5-15%), poor overnight recovery, and temperatures 5-15°F above normal, reaching near 110°F in California's central valleys. Isolated to scattered mixed wet/dry thunderstorms are expected from the eastern Sierra across the central/southern Great Basin into Utah, Colorado, and the Southwest. The driest thunderstorms with the highest ignition potential will impact central Nevada, northern Utah, and northwest Colorado. Isolated mixed wet/dry thunderstorms re anticipated in parts of Wyoming (especially the northeast), southeast Montana, and Colorado’s Front Range. Thunderstorms in the lower Mississippi Valley and Southeast should be more isolated than Sunday. Alaska’s eastern Interior remains warm and mainly dry with moderate potential in the Upper Tanana Valley. Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands continue to experience hot, dry, and breezy conditions.
