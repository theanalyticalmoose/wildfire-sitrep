SPC Day 1 Fire Weather Outlook

Issued 1123 AM CDT Fri Sep 25 2026  ·  Valid 251700Z - 261200Z  ·  Mirrored live from spc.noaa.gov
No Risk Areas Forecast. The outlook delineates no Critical or Elevated risk areas.

No changes were made to the existing fire weather outlook as a broad elevated fire weather threat commences over the northern Great Basin. West-southwest winds ahead of a cold front currently moving through northern CA into eastern OR will accelerate through the early afternoon hours across much of northern NV into adjacent southeastern OR and southwestern ID. Ample insolation aside from a few high clouds will promote a well-mixed boundary layer by mid-afternoon leading to west-southwest winds of 15-20 mph with higher gusts. These winds combined with dry conditions including RH as low as 15% will support an elevated fire weather threat over sufficiently dry fuels. A more limited fire weather risk is expected across far northwestern NV into southeastern OR as the front progresses southeastward through the day ushering in a west-northwest wind shift, increasing surface dewpoints/RH and additional cloud cover.
