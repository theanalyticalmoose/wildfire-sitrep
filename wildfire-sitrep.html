Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Hot temperatures will continue across the Plains today with highs near 100°F amid minimum RH of 15-25% and locally breezy south winds gusting to 20-25 mph. Tropical Storm Edouard will make landfall near the mouth of the Sabine River this evening bringing locally heavy rain to southeast Texas and far southwest Louisiana along with gusty winds. Scattered to widespread thunderstorms will also develop across much of Florida while a cold front moves through the Lower Great Lakes and Northeast with showers and thunderstorms. In the West, breezy and dry southwest winds will develop in much of western and southern Nevada into far northeast California and southeast Oregon creating elevated conditions as minimum relative humidity falls to 7-15%. Farther east, scattered showers and thunderstorms will develop in far northeast Oregon to central Idaho and southwest Montana then southward into the central Rockies, while isolated thunderstorms develop in the Southwest. Much cooler temperatures are expected west of the Cascades and Sierra today due to strong onshore flow.
