Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

8/3/2026 The western portion of a weakening cold front will stall in the central Great Basin into the central Rockies. Breezy west winds with gusts up to 25-35 mph and RH between 4-15% will create elevated to locally critical conditions. Lighter winds will continue in the Northwest and expand to the northern Rockies, with north winds in the Okanogan Valley (WA) and potentially in far northern California. Temperatures will be cooler than average across the Inland Northwest, Northern Rockies, and northern Great Basin. However, it will remain very hot—exceeding 100°F in lower elevations—with very low RH across California, the southern Great Basin, West Slope, and much of the Southwest. The southern Plains will also experience extreme heat at or above 105°F, while cooler-than-normal temperatures will persist east of the Mississippi River. In the Southwest, an increase in monsoon moisture may boost the coverage of wet thunderstorms over southeast Arizona and southwest through northeast New Mexico. Isolated to scattered showers and mixed wet/dry thunderstorms can be expected for Colorado, northern and eastern Wyoming and Montana, especially east of the Divide. The eastern one-third of the U.S. will have additional rounds moderate to heavy rain in scattered to numerous thunderstorms.
