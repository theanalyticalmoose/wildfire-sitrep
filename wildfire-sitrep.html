Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Widespread elevated to critical conditions will develop again today across the Inland Northwest into the northern Rockies and northwestern Great Basin today from westerly winds 15-25 mph with gusts 25-35 mph amid relative humidity of 5-20%. These breezy and dry conditions will spread into the central Great Basin later today, as well. Hot temperatures near 100°F will continue in much of the Great Basin, central Rockies, and northern Rockies east of the Divide with low afternoon humidity. Isolated mixed wet and dry thunderstorms will develop across southern Montana and Wyoming this afternoon capable of several new ignitions. Hot and dry conditions are expected in much of central and southern California, while isolated wet monsoon thunderstorms are expected in the Southwest, with mixed wet and dry thunderstorms in Colorado. Farther east, breezy conditions and low relative humidity and hot temperatures will support elevated fire weather conditions across the southern High Plains as relative humidity falls as low as 15%. In Minnesota, breezy southerly winds will develop but relative humidity is higher near 40%, with isolated to scattered wet thunderstorms developing overnight. Isolated to scattered showers and thunderstorms will develop in the Great Lakes to the Southeast, with scattered to numerous wet thunderstorms in Florida likely to temporarily ease conditions there.
