Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Southerly winds up to 35 mph, unrelenting extreme heat, and 15-30% RH will create elevated to locally critical fire conditions in the southern Plains afternoon. Very hot, dry, and moderately breezy conditions will expand across the Lower Mississippi River Valley, northern Gulf Coast, Florida, and the southeast Atlantic coast. Isolated mixed wet/dry thunderstorms are possible in the High Plains, including the Texas and Oklahoma panhandles. Highest confidence in dry lightning is expected from northwest California and the southern Cascades east across Oregon into far western Idaho. Isolated to scattered mixed wet/dry thunderstorms will impact the rest of the Northwest, northern California, northwest Nevada, Idaho, and far western Montana. Wetter thunderstorms will be scattered in the central/southern Great Basin, western Montana, much of Wyoming, Colorado, and the Southwest. RH will continue to slowly moderate in the West (minima 15-35% for most areas) with near or below- normal temperatures. Alaska’s eastern Interior will remain very warm and mostly dry with isolated thunderstorms. Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands continue to experience high-risk significant fire potential due to very warm, dry conditions and stronger winds the next two days.
