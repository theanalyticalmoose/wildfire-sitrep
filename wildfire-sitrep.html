Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Strong high pressure will deliver another very hot day across the Southern Plains and southeast US with high temperatures in the mid 90s to lower 100s. The heat will combine with minimum RHs in the mid-teens to mid 20s for much of Texas, southern Oklahoma, and a small portion of southern Arkansas and northern Louisiana resulting in a high risk burn environment. Scattered thunderstorms are also expected to develop around the periphery of the ridge including portions of Oklahoma, Arkansas, and much of the southeast US. Across the western half of the US, subtle warming will ensue, especially across the Pacific Northwest, while a weak upper- level disturbance tracks through northern California during the afternoon. This feature will bring gusty winds and low relative humidity values over northeast California, northern Nevada, southeast Oregon, into southern Idaho. This disturbance could also trigger some thunderstorms, especially over eastern Idaho into western Montana. Finally, Tropical Storm Lala will move west of the Hawaiian Islands bringing much lighter winds and a gradual end to the heavy precipitation.
