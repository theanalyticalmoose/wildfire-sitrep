Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Isolated to scattered mixed wet/dry thunderstorms are expected from Idaho into western Montana and the northern Great Basin, bringing high-risk significant fire potential to those parts. Breezy westerly winds with gusts 25-35mph amid RH 10-20% and temperatures broadly 10-15°F above normal will develop across the northern half of the West away from the coast, bringing further high-risk significant fire potential to the northern Great Basing, inland Pacific Northwest, and the Sierra Front. In the Greater Four Corners, meager moisture will yield hot temperatures, minimum RH of 10-25%, and isolated mixed wet/dry thunderstorms. Overall moderate fire potential is increasing across this region and inland southern California as the monsoon wanes and overnight RH recovery trends downward. Across the southern Plains, very hot, dry and breezy conditions will continue across east Texas into Louisiana, resulting in a critical conditions. Moderate significant fire potential will persist across remaining parts of the south including southern Florida.
