Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Moderate to high-risk significant fire potential is forecast across the southern Plains and Southern Mississippi Valley today with highs reaching 100°F and locally breezy winds, extending over Oklahoma and Arkansas amid RH of 25-30%. Moderate conditions continue across parts of Wyoming and southeastern Montana due to continued dry and breezy conditions. Across the remainder of the west, little to no risk of significant fire potential continues due to cooler conditions and recent rainfall across the northwest.

SPC Day 1 Fire Weather Outlook

Issued 1138 AM CDT Mon Sep 07 2026  ·  Valid 071700Z - 081200Z  ·  Mirrored live from spc.noaa.gov
No Risk Areas Forecast. The outlook delineates no Critical or Elevated risk areas.

Morning satellite imagery portrays thunderstorms across the Dakotas and a very dry airmass trailing an eastward progressing cold front currently in central Wyoming. 16z surface observations exhibit 15-25 mph sustained west-southwesterly winds and gusts up to 40 mph in the Wyoming plains. In the wake of a departing surface low, dry and breezy conditions will continue to permeate the northern Plains this afternoon. Despite spotty accumulating rainfall over the last 12-24 hours in far eastern Wyoming and southwestern South Dakota, ERCs broadly remain at the 80-95th percentile, maintaining an Elevated fire weather threat across the region. Only minor adjustments were made to the outlook area to exclude less-receptive fuels; see the previous discussion for more information.
