Fire Weather

Predictive Services Discussion

Breezy west winds 10-20 mph with gusts 20-30 mph will develop across the Columbia Basin and northeast Washington amid RH of 15-25% creating areas of elevated conditions, primarily through the Cascade gaps. Temperatures will be cooler today and closer to normal in the northern half of the West, while temperatures remain slightly above normal in the Southwest and minimum relative humidity remains very low at 4-12% in Nevada to southwest Idaho. Smoke will still affect many areas in the northern half of the West but be less than the past two days. Isolated wet thunderstorms will develop in the Southwest today, with scattered showers and thunderstorms develop in the Colorado Rockies east of the Divide into the central Plains. Farther east, another day of breezy south winds, low relative humidity, and hot temperatures will support locally elevated fire weather conditions across the southern High Plains. Hot conditions will continue in the Lower Mississippi Valley and into the Southeast, while isolated showers and thunderstorms develop in South Georgia and Florida. Widespread wetting rain will fall in the Northeast, with the rain heaviest near the Catskills where flooding is possible. Increased winds are expected near the Hawai’i as Tropical Storm Fausto passes to the north of the islands.

SPC Day 1 Fire Weather Outlook

Issued 1234 AM CDT Tue Jul 28 2026  ·  Valid 281200Z - 291200Z  ·  Mirrored live from spc.noaa.gov
No Risk Areas Forecast. The outlook delineates no Critical or Elevated risk areas.

West-southwest sustained winds of 10-20 mph with gusts of 20-30 mph will develop across portions of the Inland Northwest ahead of an approaching cold front and under strengthening flow aloft. RH will fall to 20-35% across central/eastern Washington and northern Oregon where winds are forecast to be the strongest leading to locally elevated conditions. Winds will shift to west-northwest behind the front in the evening and overnight as it pushes southeast across the region. However, RH is likely to increase behind the front by 5-15%, with better RH recovery overnight.

Locally elevated conditions are also expected across portions of the western/northern Great Basin into the northern Rockies with west-southwest winds of 10-20 mph amid minimum RH of 8-20%. While deeper, more widespread daytime mixing is expected across Nevada, smoke will likely inhibit mixing farther north where stronger flow aloft exists.
